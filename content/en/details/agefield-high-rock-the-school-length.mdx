## Main Story Length

Expect roughly **8–10 hours** for the main coming-of-age story. Time varies with school attendance, failed stealth or combat attempts, exploration, shopping, and how many optional activities you complete before the finale.

## Mission Count

| Content | Confirmed amount | Time impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Main missions | 32 | Forms the complete Rock the School campaign |
| Side missions and activities | 15 | Adds cash, story context, and achievement progress |
| Endings | 2 | A pre-prom save avoids a full second campaign |
| Lesson types | 5 | English, Math, Geography, German, and Music |

## Side Missions and Completion Time

A story-focused run fits the 8–10 hour estimate. A broader completion run will take longer because it adds all 15 activities, 11 Steam achievements, bike unlocks, the $1,000 savings goal, and collectible sets. The game does not publish one guaranteed 100% time because route efficiency and collectible cleanup vary.

## Download Size and Storage

The PC listing requires **15 GB of available space** and an **SSD**. Treat 15 GB as the minimum free-space requirement, not a promise that every installed build will occupy exactly that amount after updates, shader caches, save data, or storefront overhead.

## Length FAQ

### How long does Agefield High take to beat?

About 8–10 hours for a typical main-story playthrough.

### How many missions are there?

There are 32 main missions and 15 side missions or activities.

### How large is the download?

The PC requirements list 15 GB of available space and require an SSD.

### Do I need a second full playthrough for both endings?

Not if you make a manual save before the prom choice and reload it after completing the first route.
