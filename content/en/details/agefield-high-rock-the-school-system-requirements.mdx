## Gameplay Mechanics and Technical Features

*Agefield High: Rock the School* focuses on the life of Sam, a new student who arrives in town just as graduation approaches. The game’s technical performance will likely be tied to how it handles its specific simulation elements, such as the school schedule and the AI of teachers and police.

### The Time and School System
The game features a structured time system where players are expected to attend classes during the afternoon on weekdays. Players can choose to be a model student or a rebel. This system includes five different lesson types:
*   English
*   Math
*   Geography
*   German
*   Music

### Customization and Activities
While it is not a deep RPG, the game allows for character customization that may impact how the game renders Sam. You can change Sam's outfits, hairstyles, and even give him tattoos. The world also includes shops where you can purchase bikes, accessories, and other items using pocket money earned from good grades or side missions.

### Mission Structure
The game is built around a specific narrative arc called the "Rock the School" plan, involving Sam and his new friends, Kale and Axel. The technical structure of the game supports:
*   32 Main Missions
*   Two different endings based on player choices
*   15 Side Missions and activities

## Content and Maturity Ratings

Before considering the installation of *Agefield High*, players should be aware of the mature themes present in the game. The developers have modeled the experience after "raunchy teen comedies" from the early 2000s, such as *American Pie*.
