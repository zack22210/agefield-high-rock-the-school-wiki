## Understanding the Scope of Agefield High

When evaluating whether your PC will be able to run *Agefield High: Rock the School*, it is important to consider the game's design philosophy. The developers at Refugium Games have explicitly stated that the town of Agefield is a smaller, handcrafted open world. It is not intended to compete with the sprawling, resource-intensive environments found in massive studio releases.

Because the game utilizes "simple, old-school gameplay" and combat that avoids complex, fifty-button combo systems, the technical overhead may be lower than that of high-fidelity action RPGs. However, the game does feature a "Complex Time and School System" and a seamless environment including the school campus, two residential neighborhoods, a town center, and the countryside.

## Gameplay Mechanics and Technical Features

*Agefield High: Rock the School* focuses on the life of Sam, a new student who arrives in town just as graduation approaches. The game’s technical performance will likely be tied to how it handles its specific simulation elements, such as the school schedule and the AI of teachers and police.

### The Time and School System
The game features a structured time system where players are expected to attend classes during the afternoon on weekdays. Players can choose to be a model student or a rebel. This system includes five different lesson types:
*   English
*   Math
*   Geography
*   German
*   Music

### Customization and Activities
While it is not a deep RPG, the game allows for character customization that may impact how the game renders Sam. You can change Sam's outfits, hairstyles, and even give him tattoos. The world also includes shops where you can purchase bikes, accessories, and other items using pocket money earned from good grades or side missions.

### Mission Structure
The game is built around a specific narrative arc called the "Rock the School" plan, involving Sam and his new friends, Kale and Axel. The technical structure of the game supports:
*   32 Main Missions
*   Two different endings based on player choices
*   15 Side Missions and activities

## Content and Maturity Ratings

Before considering the installation of *Agefield High*, players should be aware of the mature themes present in the game. The developers have modeled the experience after "raunchy teen comedies" from the early 2000s, such as *American Pie*. 

The game contains the following content:
*   **Humor:** Edgy and crude humor that the developers describe as potentially "outdated" or "offensive."
*   **Violence:** Mild violence without blood.
*   **Themes:** References to alcohol consumption, sexual innuendo, sexual harassment, and partial nudity.
*   **Sensitive Subjects:** References to suicide.

These elements are central to the game's identity as a period-accurate tribute to the "American Pie-era."

## FAQ

### Is Agefield High: Rock the School coming to consoles?
The Windows PC version is released, and a PS5 version is announced with its date still TBD. Xbox and Nintendo versions are not currently confirmed.

### Does the game have multiplayer or co-op?
No. *Agefield High* is strictly a single-player experience from beginning to end. There are no cooperative or competitive multiplayer modes included.

### How long does it take to beat Agefield High: Rock the School?
The main story is designed to last roughly 8 to 10 hours. This includes 32 main missions, though players can extend their playtime by engaging in the 15 side missions and various school activities.

### Can I run this game on a low-end PC?
Compare your PC with the minimum tier: Ryzen 5 2600X or Core i7-7700K, 16 GB RAM, GTX 1070 8 GB or RX 580 8 GB, DirectX 12, and an SSD. Hardware below any of those points is outside the published minimum and may not provide a reliable experience.
