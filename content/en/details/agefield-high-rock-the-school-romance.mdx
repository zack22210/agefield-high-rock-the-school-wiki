## Ashley Romance

Ashley is introduced as the head cheerleader and Seth's girlfriend. Sam's early house dare involves her home, but that task is not itself the romance branch. Continue the main story, then choose Ashley when the prom-date decision appears. Read [Ashley’s character guide](/characters/ashley) for her missions and relationship context.

## Chloe Romance

Chloe's relationship context develops through social missions such as the prank call and guitar sequence. Choose Officer Drebin during the call to get past her father, then complete the later story objectives leading to prom. Choosing Chloe at the final branch produces her outcome.

## Prom Choice

The prom choice is the most clearly documented split between the two routes. Create a manual save immediately before selecting Ashley or Chloe. Complete one prom mission, confirm the achievement, then reload the save to choose the other route.

| Choice | Outcome | Achievement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Ashley | Ashley prom ending | Ashley's Sweet Boy Sam |
| Chloe | Chloe prom ending | Chloe's Little Guitar Hero |

## Ending Impact

Agefield High has two confirmed endings. Available evidence does not support a hidden third route or an officially designated true ending. Side missions, grades, collectibles, and other decisions may add context or completion progress, but they should not be presented as verified romance-point requirements without documented proof.

Use the [canonical endings guide](/guide/agefield-high-rock-the-school-ashley-ending) for route preparation and the [complete walkthrough](/guide) for the story sequence.

## Other Relationships

Sam's friendships with Kale and Axel drive the Rock the School plan. Seth's relationship with Ashley and rivalry with Sam shape the early conflict. Chloe's connection to Hugh also matters during the Bad Grades setup. Browse the [characters guide](/details/agefield-high-rock-the-school-characters) for the full cast and relationship table.

## Romance FAQ

### Who can Sam choose for prom?

Ashley or Chloe. Each has a confirmed ending achievement.

### Is there a relationship meter?

No verified requirement for a visible relationship-point system is documented in the available material. Follow the story missions and preserve a save before prom.

### Which romance is the true ending?

Neither. No separate true ending is officially confirmed.

### Can I see both endings without replaying all 32 missions?

Yes, if you make a manual save immediately before the prom choice and reload it after the first ending.
