## What Is Agefield High: Rock the School?

Developed by Refugium Games and released on August 12, 2026, *Agefield High: Rock the School* is a single-player, open-world action-adventure game [2]. The story follows a new student named Sam who moves to the town of Agefield just months before his high school graduation [2].

The core gameplay loop centers on helping Sam’s new friends, Kale and Axel, execute their "Rock the School" plan [2]. This plan involves:
*   Confronting the school's jocks.
*   Organizing a massive party.
*   Securing a date for the prom [2].

While players and content creators have frequently drawn comparisons between this title and *Bully* due to the school-based setting and social dynamics, it is important to clarify that *Agefield High* is an independent project and not an official successor or sequel to any existing franchise [2].

## Gameplay and Features

*Agefield High: Rock the School* offers a structured experience set in the early 2000s [2]. The game features a time and school attendance system, requiring players to balance their social ambitions with academic responsibilities [2].

### Key Game Mechanics
*   **Open-World Exploration:** The game features a small open-world environment that includes the high school campus, two distinct neighborhoods, the town center, and the surrounding countryside [2].
*   **Mission Structure:** The game contains 32 main missions that lead to one of two different endings, alongside 15 side missions and activities [2].
*   **Educational Mini-games:** Players must attend classes, with five different lesson types available: English, Math, Geography, German, and Music [2].
*   **Customization:** Shops throughout the town allow players to customize Sam’s appearance, including his clothes, hairstyles, and tattoos [2].
*   **Transportation:** Players can acquire and ride bikes, which can be managed via a garage system [2].

## Understanding the Gameplay Experience

Early gameplay walkthroughs highlight that the game emphasizes social navigation and stealth mechanics [2]. For instance, early missions involve navigating school hierarchies and performing "dares" to gain status among peers [2]. 

Combat is a component of the game, often triggered during social confrontations. Players utilize basic mechanics such as punching and blocking to defend themselves or stand up to bullies [2]. Because the game is a single-player adventure, it does not feature online multiplayer modes [2].
