## Sam

Sam is the player-controlled new student. With only a few months before graduation, he balances classes, money, exploration, fights, friendships, and the crew's plan. Players can change his clothes, hair, tattoos, and accessories, but the central story remains the same 32-mission campaign.

## Kale

Kale is one of Sam's core friends and co-conspirators. Alongside Axel, he tests Sam's nerve and keeps the Rock the School plan moving. He belongs to the friendship and mission intent rather than a separate romance route.

## Axel

Axel is the aspiring rock musician Sam meets during the first day. He helps introduce the school hierarchy, brings Sam into the crew, and remains connected to the late-stage plan. Some decisions around the final event concern whether Axel gets to perform his set.

## Ashley

Ashley is the head cheerleader, Seth's girlfriend early in the story, and one of Sam's two confirmed prom choices. Generic Ashley questions belong on the [Ashley entity page](/characters/ashley); route-specific questions belong in the [endings guide](/guide/agefield-high-rock-the-school-ashley-ending).

## Chloe

Chloe is the goth student at the center of the prank-call and guitar sequence. Her social missions build the context for choosing her at prom. Completing her final route unlocks Chloe's Little Guitar Hero.

## Seth

Seth is the school bully and Sam's first major combat opponent. The encounter after English class introduces blocking, countering, pushing, punches, and kicks. He also influences Ashley's early relationship context and later conflicts involving other students.

## Laura

Laura appears in a playground rescue sequence where Sam intervenes against jocks. Helping her changes the social context around Sam and opens further interaction with the school's outsiders.

## Miss Davidson

Miss Davidson teaches English and introduces the first spelling exercise. Her class is where Sam meets several central students. See the [walkthrough's class answers](/guide#class-answers) for the verified word list.

## Hugh

Hugh is Chloe's younger brother. Sam meets him near the outskirts during the setup for Bad Grades and collects the pre-graded papers needed for the night infiltration.

## Romance Characters

Ashley and Chloe are the two confirmed prom outcomes. Their stories develop during the same campaign, and the clearest branch occurs at the prom decision. Use the [romance guide](/details/agefield-high-rock-the-school-romance) for the relationship sequences and the [endings guide](/guide/agefield-high-rock-the-school-ashley-ending) for final-route preparation.

## Teachers

Teachers give the school schedule practical weight. Miss Davidson runs English, while other staff supervise lessons or patrol restricted areas. During Bad Grades, Mr. Haley and a security guard create the mission's detection and failure pressure.

## Other NPCs

The town includes Mike at the bike shop, Sergeant Clint, the bag thief and the woman Sam helps, jocks, police, shopkeepers, and mission-specific residents. These NPCs provide transport, cash, chores, combat, shopping, or enforcement rather than confirmed branching romance routes.

## Relationships

- **Sam, Kale, and Axel:** the friendship at the center of the Rock the School plan.
- **Ashley and Seth:** an established relationship at the beginning of the story.
- **Sam and Ashley / Chloe:** the late prom choice creates the two confirmed outcomes.
- **Chloe and Hugh:** siblings who connect the social story to the Bad Grades mission.
- **Sam and Seth:** a rivalry established by the first-day fight.

## Character FAQ

### Who is the main character?

You play as Sam, a new student who joins Kale and Axel's plan before graduation.

### Who are the romance options?

Ashley and Chloe are the two confirmed prom outcomes.

### Are there two endings?

Yes. Use a manual save before the prom choice to see both without replaying the whole campaign.
