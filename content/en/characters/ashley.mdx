## Who Is Ashley?

Ashley is Agefield High's head cheerleader and one of the central students Sam meets during his first English class. Her position in the school hierarchy and her relationship with Seth place her close to the conflict driving Sam, Kale, and Axel's Rock the School plan.

## Role in the Story

Sam first sees Ashley alongside Seth during the opening school sequence. Soon afterward, Kale and Axel challenge Sam to enter Ashley's house as a test of nerve. That early mission treats her as part of the crew's rebellion plot; later choices give her a more direct place in Sam's personal story.

## Ashley and Seth

Ashley begins the story as **Seth's girlfriend**. Seth is the school bully and an early combat opponent, so Sam's interactions with Ashley are tied to an existing rivalry. The relationship context matters when reading romance or ending advice, but it does not make the early house dare an ending trigger by itself.

## Ashley Missions

The clearest early Ashley-focused task is the house dare:

1. Follow the waypoint to Ashley's residence.
2. Circle to the backyard and pool rather than using the front door.
3. Crouch through the rear entrance and go upstairs.
4. Search the pink bedroom area for the mission item.
5. Escape through the yard and win the required fight.

Use the [complete walkthrough](/guide) for the full sequence and the [interactive map](/details/agefield-high-rock-the-school-map) for route planning.

## Ashley Romance Route

Ashley's route develops through the main story rather than a separate dating-simulator menu. Continue the campaign, complete the required story missions, and preserve a manual save before the late prom decision. The available ending evidence confirms the final choice and achievement; it does not support inventing extra relationship-point requirements.

## Prom Choice

At the late prom branch, choose **Ashley** if you want her outcome. Save immediately before committing to a date so you can reload and choose Chloe afterward without replaying all 32 main missions.

## Ashley Ending

Completing the prom mission with Ashley produces one of the game's two confirmed endings. For the comparison with Chloe, route preparation, and the clarification that there is no separately confirmed true ending, use the [Ashley vs Chloe endings guide](/guide/agefield-high-rock-the-school-ashley-ending).

## Ashley Achievement

Finishing the prom mission on Ashley's branch unlocks **Ashley's Sweet Boy Sam**. It is one of 11 Steam achievements; the remaining set covers Chloe's route, savings, bikes, side activities, and collectibles.

## Ashley FAQ

### Who is Ashley in Agefield High?

Ashley is the head cheerleader, Seth's girlfriend at the start of the story, and a possible prom choice for Sam.

### Can Sam choose Ashley for prom?

Yes. Ashley is one of the two confirmed prom outcomes; the other is Chloe.

### What achievement does the Ashley ending unlock?

Completing the prom mission with Ashley unlocks **Ashley's Sweet Boy Sam**.

### Is Ashley's house mission the ending choice?

No. It is an early story dare. The ending branch occurs later at the prom choice.
