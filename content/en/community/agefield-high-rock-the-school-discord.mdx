## About Agefield High: Rock the School

*Agefield High: Rock the School* is a coming-of-age adventure that transports players back to the early 2000s. You take on the role of Sam, a new student at Agefield High School, tasked with helping your new friends, Kale and Axel, execute their plan to "Rock the School" before graduation.

The game is designed as a single-player, open-world experience. Refugium Games has crafted a setting that includes the school campus, two neighborhoods, the town center, and the surrounding countryside. With 8–10 hours of expected gameplay, the title focuses on themes of friendship, rebellion, and the classic high school experience.

## Gameplay Features and Mechanics

To help you navigate the halls of Agefield High, it is useful to understand the core systems built into the game. The experience is structured around a time and school attendance system, requiring you to balance your rebellious adventures with your academic responsibilities.

### Core Gameplay Breakdown

*   **Mission Structure:** The game features 32 main missions that lead to one of two different endings, depending on your choices throughout the journey.
*   **Side Content:** Beyond the main story, there are 15 side missions and activities available to explore.
*   **Academic Life:** You will attend five distinct lesson types: English, Math, Geography, German, and Music.
*   **Customization:** Players can visit various shops to purchase bikes, clothing items, hairstyles, tattoos, and accessories. You can even find "dirty magazines" while exploring the town.
