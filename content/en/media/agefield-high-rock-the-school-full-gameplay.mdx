With the release of **Agefield High: Rock the School** on August 12, 2026, players are diving into the early 2000s nostalgia of Refugium Games’ latest title. As a new student named Sam, players must navigate the social hierarchies of Agefield High while assisting friends Kale and Axel in their quest to "Rock the School" and leave a lasting legacy.

## Gameplay Overview and Length

Agefield High: Rock the School is a single-player coming-of-age adventure that blends social simulation with open-world exploration. According to the developer, a standard playthrough focusing on the main story and various side activities typically lasts between **8 to 10 hours**.

The game is set in the early 2000s, drawing heavy inspiration from the raunchy teen comedies of that era. Players experience a "Time & School System," which requires Sam to manage his schedule, attend classes, and interact with the environment during specific windows of the day. Unlike traditional linear adventures, the game offers a level of freedom in how you approach your final days before graduation.

## Main Missions and Story Progression

The core gameplay revolves around Sam’s integration into his new school and his involvement with Kale and Axel. The primary goal is to execute the "Rock the School" plan, win over Sam's dream girl, and navigate the typical rebellions of high school life.

*   **Total Main Missions:** 32
*   **Endings:** There are two different endings confirmed for the main story.

While the specific names and triggers for these two endings have not been detailed in official documentation, the developer notes that the choices made during these 32 missions determine Sam's ultimate reputation and the outcome of the graduation season. At this time, a step-by-step walkthrough for every mission is not available in the official source material, but progression is tied to both social interactions and successful completion of school-related tasks.

## The School System and Lessons

A significant portion of the gameplay takes place within the walls of Agefield High School. The school system is not merely a backdrop; it is a functional part of the gameplay loop that includes mandatory attendance and academic performance.

Players can participate in five different lesson types. Successfully navigating these lessons is part of the daily routine:

| Lesson Type | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **English** | Standard academic coursework. |
| **Math** | Core curriculum requirement. |
| **Geography** | Exploration of world locations. |
| **German** | Foreign language studies. |
| **Music** | Directly relevant to the "Rock the School" theme. |

Outside of these lessons, the "Time System" dictates when certain missions become available and when shops or specific areas of the town are accessible.

## Exploring the Town of Agefield

Beyond the campus, Agefield High: Rock the School features a small open world. Players are free to explore these areas between classes or during their free time to complete side missions and find collectibles.

The open world is divided into four primary sectors:
1.  **The School Campus:** The central hub for missions and social interaction.
2.  **Neighborhoods:** Two distinct residential areas where Sam and his peers live.
3.  **Town Center:** The commercial heart of Agefield, containing various shops.
4.  **Countryside:** The outskirts of town, offering a change of pace from the suburban environment.

## Side Missions and Activities

For players looking to extend their gameplay beyond the 32 main missions, there are **15 side missions and activities** scattered throughout Agefield. These activities often involve helping classmates or engaging in the "rebellious" behavior inspired by the teen comedy genre.

The town center and various neighborhoods house shops where Sam can spend money earned or found during his adventures. Customization is a key component of the gameplay, allowing players to modify Sam’s appearance and gear.

**Available items in shops include:**
*   **Vehicles:** Bikes for faster travel across the open world.
*   **Appearance:** New clothes, hairstyles, and tattoos.
*   **Accessories:** Various items to customize Sam's look.
*   **Misc Items:** "Dirty magazines" and other era-appropriate items found in local shops.
