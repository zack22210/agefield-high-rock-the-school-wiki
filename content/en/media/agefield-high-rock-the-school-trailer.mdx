The narrative follows a new student named Sam who arrives in Agefield just as graduation is approaching. Your primary objective involves joining forces with two friends, Kale and Axel, to execute their ambitious "Rock the School" plan.

The gameplay experience is built around an 8–10 hour narrative arc.
