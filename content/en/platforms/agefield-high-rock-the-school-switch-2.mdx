## Gameplay and the Agefield Open World

*Agefield High: Rock the School* places players in the role of Sam, a new student at Agefield High School. The game is set in the early 2000s, a period defined by specific fashion, music, and the "raunchy teen comedy" aesthetic that inspired the developers. The narrative follows Sam as he teams up with two new friends, Kale and Axel, to execute their "Rock the School" plan before graduation.

The game features a "small open world," which is more focused than sprawling triple-A titles but packed with interactive zones. The map is divided into four primary areas:
*   **The Agefield High School Campus:** The central hub where Sam attends classes and interacts with the student body.
*   **Neighborhoods:** Residential areas where the characters live and where various side activities may take place.
*   **The Town Center:** A commercial hub featuring shops and social spots.
*   **The Countryside:** The outskirts of town, offering a change of pace from the suburban and school environments.

Unlike some linear adventures, the world is free to explore between main missions and scheduled classes. This freedom allows players to immerse themselves in the early 2000s setting at their own pace.

## The School and Time System

A core mechanic of *Agefield High: Rock the School* is the Time & School System. This requires players to manage Sam’s schedule, including mandatory school attendance. This system ensures that the "coming-of-age" theme is reflected in the gameplay, as Sam must balance his rebellious plans with the requirements of being a student.

Education in Agefield isn't just a backdrop; it involves five distinct lesson types that players must navigate:
1.  **English**
2.  **Math**
3.  **Geography**
4.  **German**
5.  **Music**

These lessons represent the academic side of Sam's life, contrasting with the "Rock the School" rebellion he plots with Kale and Axel. The inclusion of Music lessons is particularly thematic given the game's title and the goal to "leave your mark as a legend."
