## What Is Agefield High: Rock the School?

*Agefield High: Rock the School* is a single-player, coming-of-age adventure that leans into the nostalgia of the early 2000s. Players take on the role of Sam, a new student at Agefield High School. The narrative centers on friendship, love, and rebellion as you assist your new friends, Kale and Axel, in executing their plan to "Rock the School" and secure your status as a local legend.

The game features a small open-world environment that includes:
*   The Agefield High School campus
*   Two distinct neighborhoods
*   The town center
*   The surrounding countryside

Beyond the main story, the game integrates a time and school attendance system, requiring players to balance their social ambitions with their academic responsibilities.

## Gameplay Features and Content

The game offers a structured experience designed to immerse players in the high school life of the early 2000s. Whether you are attending classes or exploring the town, there are several systems in place to keep the gameplay engaging:

*   **Main Missions:** There are 32 main missions to complete, which feature two different potential endings.
*   **Side Content:** Players can engage in 15 side missions and various activities scattered throughout the town.
*   **Academic Life:** You will attend five different types of lessons, covering English, Math, Geography, German, and Music.
*   **Customization and Shopping:** The game includes shops where you can purchase bikes, clothing, hairstyles, tattoos, and various accessories, including dirty magazines.

The small open world is free to explore between classes and mission objectives, allowing you to move through the school campus, neighborhoods, and countryside at your own pace.
