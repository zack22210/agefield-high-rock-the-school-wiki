## Gameplay and Story: Sam’s Journey

*Agefield High: Rock the School* is inspired by the raunchy teen comedies of the early 2000s. Players take on the role of Sam, a new student arriving at Agefield High just as graduation approaches. The narrative centers on Sam’s attempts to navigate the social hierarchy, find romance, and assist his new friends, Kale and Axel, in executing their "Rock the School" plan.

The story is structured around a "Time & School System," which requires players to balance their rebellious activities with actual school attendance. This system tethers the player to the school's schedule, creating a loop of academic responsibility and after-hours exploration.

### Key Narrative Elements:
*   **Friendship and Rebellion:** Help Kale and Axel leave their mark as legends before graduation.
*   **Romance:** Pursue a "dream girl" subplot integrated into the main story.
*   **Branching Paths:** The game features 32 main missions with two distinct endings, though the specific requirements for these endings have not been disclosed in official materials.

## Exploring the Town of Agefield

The game features a small open world that players are free to explore when they aren't attending class or pursuing main missions. The environment is designed to evoke the aesthetic of the early 2000s, covering several distinct zones.

### Map Locations:
1.  **Agefield High School Campus:** The central hub where most missions and lessons take place.
2.  **Neighborhoods:** Residential areas where Sam and his peers live.
3.  **Town Center:** A commercial hub containing various shops and social spots.
4.  **Countryside:** The outskirts of town, offering more space for exploration away from the school faculty.

While the world is described as a "small open-world," it is densely packed with 15 side missions and various activities to fill the 8–10 hours of gameplay.
