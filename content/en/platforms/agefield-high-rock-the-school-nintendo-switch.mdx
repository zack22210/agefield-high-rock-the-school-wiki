## What is Agefield High: Rock the School?

Agefield High: Rock the School is a single-player, coming-of-age action-adventure game. Set in the early 2000s, the story follows a new student named Sam as he navigates the final days of high school before graduation. 

The game is heavily inspired by the raunchy teen comedies of the late 90s and early 2000s. Players are tasked with helping Sam’s new friends, Kale and Axel, execute their "Rock the School" plan. The goal is simple but ambitious: rebel against the status quo, win over Sam's dream girl, and leave a permanent mark on the town of Agefield as legends.

### Core Gameplay Mechanics
The game utilizes a "Time & School System" that requires players to balance their social lives with school attendance. This adds a layer of simulation to the adventure, as Sam must manage his schedule to succeed both academically and socially.

*   **Campaign Length:** Approximately 8–10 hours of gameplay.
*   **Mission Structure:** 32 Main Missions with two different endings based on player progress and choices.
*   **Side Content:** 15 Side Missions and various activities scattered throughout the world.
*   **Academic Life:** 5 different lesson types, including English, Math, Geography, German, and Music.
