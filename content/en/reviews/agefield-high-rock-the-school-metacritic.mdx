## What is Agefield High: Rock the School?

*Agefield High: Rock the School* is a single-player coming-of-age adventure game that immerses players in the early 2000s. Developed by Refugium Games, the title is heavily inspired by the raunchy teen comedies that defined that era. 

Players step into the shoes of Sam, a new student at Agefield High School. The narrative centers on friendship, love, and rebellion as Sam teams up with new friends, Kale and Axel, to execute their "Rock the School" plan. The ultimate goal is to navigate the social and academic challenges of high school life and leave a mark as a legend before graduation.

*   **Gameplay Duration:** The main story is designed to last between 8 and 10 hours.
*   **Mission Structure:** The game features 32 main missions and 15 side missions or activities.
*   **Narrative Choices:** Players will encounter a story with two different endings. Please note that specific triggers or details regarding how to achieve these endings have not been disclosed by the developer.
*   **Open-World Exploration:** Outside of structured missions, players can explore a small open world that includes the school campus, two distinct neighborhoods, the town center, and the countryside.
*   **School Simulation:** The game incorporates a time and school attendance system, requiring players to attend classes.
*   **Academic Subjects:** There are five different lesson types: English, Math, Geography, German, and Music.
