## Exploring Agefield for Completionists

Players looking to see everything the game has to offer before hitting the finale should take advantage of the small open world. Beyond the 32 main missions, there are 15 side missions and various activities that flesh out the town of Agefield.

Verified locations you can explore include:
*   **Agefield High School Campus:** The hub for most main missions and all five lesson types.
*   **Two Neighborhoods:** Areas where Sam and his friends likely reside.
*   **Town Center:** Home to various shops where you can buy bikes, clothes, and accessories.
*   **The Countryside:** An outlying area for further exploration.

Exploring these areas and completing side content can provide more of Sam's story, but the official page does not connect completion percentage to either ending.
