## Verified Encounter: Seth Fight

The clearly documented named fight occurs against **Seth after the first English class**. Older wording that called an unverified encounter a “Chapter 2 boss” has been removed. This page will not create separate chapter, boss, or how-to-beat URLs until a distinct encounter is reliably documented.

## Quick Strategy

Hold block while Seth starts his attack, wait until the swing finishes, counter with two or three fast hits, then step or push away. Short punish windows are safer than a long combo. Repeat until the encounter ends.

## Seth's Attacks and Counters

| Seth's action | Response |
| :--- | :--- |
| Steps into punching range | Block and let the first swing finish |
| Continues a short string | Keep blocking; counter only after the animation ends |
| Crowds Sam near a wall | Push to create space, then move back toward open ground |
| Recovers after missing | Use a short punch-and-kick counter, then disengage |

## Failure Conditions

Sam loses when his health is depleted. Repeatedly trading hits, attacking through Seth's active swing, getting trapped by level geometry, or losing the camera in a corner makes that much more likely. The story continues after the tutorial context, but winning cleanly is useful practice for later fights.

## Step-by-Step Fight Strategy

1. Move into open space so the camera can keep both characters visible.
2. Let Seth approach rather than sprinting into his first attack.
3. Hold block through the active swing.
4. Counter with a short punch sequence and one kick.
5. Use push if Seth immediately crowds Sam.
6. Reset to mid-range and repeat the block-counter rhythm.
7. Heal before later free-roam fights by using food or soda from the inventory.

## Controls and Preparation

On the documented controller layout, **RT** punches, **B** kicks, and **X** pushes. Keep the block control held only when an attack is coming so you can still reposition. The [main walkthrough](/guide#fighting-seth) covers the story context, and the [characters guide](/details/agefield-high-rock-the-school-characters#seth) explains Seth's role.

## Fight FAQ

### Is Seth the Chapter 2 boss?

The verified walkthrough identifies the early Seth fight after English class. It does not establish a separate encounter officially named the Chapter 2 boss.

### Why do my attacks keep getting interrupted?

You are probably attacking during Seth's active swing. Block first and counter after the animation finishes.

### How do I escape a corner?

Block the next hit, use push to create space, and move back toward the center before attacking again.
