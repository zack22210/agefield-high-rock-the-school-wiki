## Ashley Ending Route

Ashley begins the story as Seth's girlfriend and the target of the early house dare, but her later relationship with Sam can culminate in the Ashley prom outcome. Progress through the 32 main missions, keep a save before the final prom decision, and choose Ashley when the date choice appears. Finishing the prom mission on this branch unlocks **Ashley's Sweet Boy Sam**.

For Ashley's story role, missions, and relationship context outside the ending task, use the [Ashley character guide](/characters/ashley).

## Chloe Ending Route

Chloe's route grows out of Sam's social missions, including the prank call and guitar sequence. Continue the main story until the prom branch, then choose Chloe. Completing the prom mission with her unlocks **Chloe's Little Guitar Hero**. The route is a parallel ending choice, not proof that one outcome is more canonical than the other.

## Prom Choice and Save Point

Create a **manual save immediately before the game asks Sam to commit to a prom date**. Finish unresolved side missions, collectible cleanup, purchases, and the $1,000 savings requirement before advancing if you want an efficient completion run. After viewing one ending and receiving its achievement, reload the pre-choice save and choose the other date.

## Ashley vs Chloe Comparison

| Route | Final choice | Ending achievement | Best preparation |

| **Ashley** | Choose Ashley for prom | Ashley's Sweet Boy Sam | Review Ashley's missions and save before the date choice |

Both routes sit inside the same 32-mission campaign. The game also has 15 side missions and activities, but available official information does not confirm that 100% side completion is required to make either prom choice.

## Ending Achievements

The two prom outcomes each have a Steam achievement. They are part of the game's **11 achievements**, alongside saving $1,000, unlocking every bicycle, completing all mini missions and activities, and finishing the collectible sets. Review the [achievements overview](/#explore-6) before committing to the finale so you do not create an unnecessary full replay.

## Is There a True Ending?

No separate, officially confirmed “true ending” is documented. The developer confirms **two endings**, and the achievement list identifies the Ashley and Chloe prom outcomes. “True ending,” “good ending,” or “secret ending” labels used by older pages or community posts should not be treated as official route names without additional verified evidence.

## Ending FAQ

### How many endings are in Agefield High: Rock the School?

There are two confirmed endings. The late prom choice produces the Ashley or Chloe outcome.

### Which ending is the true ending?

Neither route is officially designated as the true ending. Both have their own prom achievement.

### Can I unlock both endings without replaying the whole game?

Yes, if you create a manual save before the prom-date choice. Finish one route, reload the save, and choose the other date.

### Do side missions change the ending?

The game has 15 side missions and activities, but available official material does not confirm that completing all of them changes the prom branch. Complete them before the finale for rewards, story context, and the Hard Worker achievement.
