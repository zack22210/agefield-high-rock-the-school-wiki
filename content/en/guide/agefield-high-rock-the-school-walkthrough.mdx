Navigating the early 2000s as the new kid in town isn't easy, especially when graduation is just three months away. In **Agefield High: Rock the School**, developed by Refugium Games, you take on the role of Sam, a teenager trying to navigate friendship, rebellion, and romance in a town inspired by classic raunchy teen comedies. This walkthrough covers the essential steps to completing the prologue, surviving your first classes, and executing the "Bad Grades" stealth mission.

## Getting Started: The Prologue

The game begins in Sam’s bedroom. Before heading to school, you are introduced to the basic movement and customization systems.

### Controls and Customization
*   **Movement (PC):** Use **WASD** to walk, **Shift** to sprint, and **Space** to jump.
*   **Movement (Controller):** Standard analog sticks. Press **RT** to punch, **X** to push, and **B** to kick during combat.
*   **Customization:** Interact with the wardrobe in your room to change Sam's outfit. You can modify haircuts, hats, glasses, and shirts. 
*   **Objectives:** Always check the upper-left corner of the screen for your current mission objective. Press **M** to open the map and **I** to access your inventory.

After changing out of your pajamas, head downstairs. You will meet Sam’s sister and mother. The prologue concludes with a scripted drive to Agefield High, where Sam’s mother encourages him to stay on the "right path" to a normal life—a path Sam is clearly ready to rebel against.

## Surviving Your First Day at Agefield High

Upon arriving at school, your first goal is to reach your classroom. The school environment is filled with NPCs whose dialogue sets the "unhinged" tone of the early 2000s setting.

### The English Class Spelling Test
In Miss Davidson’s English class, you will be introduced to the main cast:
*   **Axel:** The aspiring rockstar and your first friend.
*   **Seth:** The school bully (often referred to as a "werewolf" due to his appearance).
*   **Ashley:** The head cheerleader and Seth’s girlfriend.
*   **Chloe:** A goth student who becomes a central interest for Sam.

Miss Davidson will administer a spelling exercise. To get a 100% score, choose the correct spelling for the following words when prompted:
1.  Miscellaneous
2.  Saucer
3.  Freedom
4.  Conscience
5.  Romanticize
6.  Session
7.  Incident
8.  Receive

### Fighting Seth
After class, a scripted confrontation occurs with Seth. This serves as a combat tutorial. 
*   **Blocking:** Hold the block button to mitigate damage.
*   **Countering:** Wait for Seth to swing, block, and then respond with a series of punches (**RT**) and kicks (**B**).
*   **Pushing:** Use **X** to create distance if you are being overwhelmed.

Regardless of the fight's outcome, Seth will give you a warning, and Axel will invite you to meet him and Kale in the park after school to discuss "The Plan."

## The Plan and The Dare: Stealing Ashley's Panties

To join Axel and Kale’s crew, you must prove your "guts" by completing a dare: stealing Ashley’s panties from her home. 

### Stealth at Ashley’s House
1.  **Locate the House:** Follow the waypoint on your map to Ashley’s residence.
2.  **Infiltration:** Do not go through the front door. Head to the backyard and locate the pool area.
3.  **Entering the House:** Use the **Right Stick** to crouch. Sneak through the back entrance.
4.  **The Pink Room:** Head upstairs to find Ashley’s room (it is decorated in pink). Interact with the laundry to find the objective.
5.  **Escape:** While leaving, you will encounter a jock "guard dog" in the backyard. You must defeat him in a fistfight to successfully exit the area.

## Exploring Agefield: Money and Bikes

Once the dare is complete, the town of Agefield opens up for exploration. To progress "The Plan," you will need supplies and money.

### Earning Cash
*   **Sergeant Clint’s Lawn:** Visit Sergeant Clint and offer to do chores. You will enter a lawn-mowing mini-game. While Clint pays in an old cassette tape rather than cash, this item is required for a later mission.
*   **The Bag Thief:** You will witness a thief stealing an elderly woman’s bag. Chase the thief down (using a bike is recommended) and recover the bag. The woman will reward you with **$50**, which is used to buy "Epiack" (vomit juice) for Seth’s upcoming party.

### Mike’s Bike Shop
Mike, the local bike shop owner, offers Sam a deal: recover a stolen bike, and he can keep it.
1.  **Follow Seth:** Locate Seth near an alley and follow him at a distance. This is a stealth mission; if you get too close, the mission fails.
2.  **The Backyard:** Seth will lead you to a house. Sneak into the backyard, avoid being spotted by the jocks, and grab the mountain bike.
3.  **The Reward:** Once returned to the garage, the bike becomes Sam’s permanent method of fast transportation.

## Social Missions: Chloe and Laura

A significant portion of the game involves Sam attempting to impress Chloe and helping other students.

### The Prank Call and Guitar Mini-game
To get Chloe’s attention, Sam must call her house. 
*   **The Choice:** When calling, choose to impersonate **Officer Drebin** to get past her father.
*   **The Serenade:** Chloe challenges Sam to play guitar outside her window. This triggers a rhythm-based mini-game. Success here improves Sam's standing with Chloe, though it ends abruptly when her father, Mr. Rogers, chases Sam away.

### Saving Laura
At the playground, you will find jocks harassing Laura, the goth girl from class. You must intervene and defeat the jocks in combat. Completing this mission changes Sam's relationship with the school's social outcasts and opens up new dialogue options.

## Mission: Bad Grades (Night Stealth)

One of the more challenging missions involves sneaking into Agefield High at night to change the group's English grades.

1.  **Meet Hugh:** Go to the outskirts to meet Chloe’s younger brother, Hugh, at his clubhouse to get the pre-graded papers.
2.  **Night Infiltration:** Enter the school after dark. You must avoid the security guard and **Mr. Haley**, who roams the halls.
3.  **Switching Papers:** 
    *   Sam’s objective is the **Teacher’s Lounge** in the lower hallway.
    *   Axel and Kale head to the Geography class upstairs.
4.  **The Escape:** Once the papers are swapped, a timer starts. You must reach the upper roof to escape. If spotted by Mr. Haley, he will shout, "No one gets pregnant on my watch!"—a reference to a school legend from the 80s. 
5.  **Hiding:** If the guards are alerted, head to the Gym and interact with the hiding spots until the heat dies down.

## Agefield High FAQ

### How do I restore health in Agefield High?
You can restore health by purchasing food or sodas from shops or vending machines. Open your inventory with **I** and consume the items to refill your health bar.

### Where can I find my bike if I lose it?
If you lose your bike while exploring the town, you can always find it back in the garage at Sam’s house. Alternatively, you can check road bike racks marked on your map.

### How long is the game?
Based on the mission structure and town exploration, a standard playthrough of *Agefield High: Rock the School* takes approximately **8 hours**, depending on how many side chores and mini-games you complete.

### Can I change Sam's appearance later in the game?
Yes. You can visit clothing shops in the town of Agefield (marked with a yellow icon) to buy new outfits, such as the "Rockstar" outfit, or change your hair and accessories.
