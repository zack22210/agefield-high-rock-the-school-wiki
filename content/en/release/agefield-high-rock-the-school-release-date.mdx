The wait is finally over for fans of coming-of-age adventures. **Agefield High: Rock the School** has officially launched, with its release date set for August 12, 2026. Developed by Refugium Games, this title invites players to step into the shoes of a new student named Sam as they navigate the trials and tribulations of high school life in the early 2000s.

## What to Expect in Agefield High
Set in the titular town of Agefield, the game captures the spirit of classic, raunchy teen comedies. As you take on the role of Sam, your primary objective is to assist your new friends, Kale and Axel, in executing their ambitious "Rock the School" plan. Along the way, you will be tasked with winning over your dream girl and establishing your own legacy within the halls of Agefield High.

The game is designed as a single-player, open-world experience that focuses on the social and academic dynamics of a student's final days before graduation. With an estimated 8–10 hours of gameplay, the title balances narrative-driven missions with the freedom to explore the surrounding environment.

## Gameplay and Features
Refugium Games has packed the experience with various systems to simulate the daily grind of a high school student. The gameplay loop is defined by a Time and School System, which requires players to attend classes regularly. 

### Core Gameplay Elements
*   **School Attendance:** Players must balance their social life and missions with mandatory lessons.
*   **Academic Subjects:** The game features five distinct lesson types: English, Math, Geography, German, and Music.
*   **Open-World Exploration:** Beyond the school campus, players can roam through two neighborhoods, the town center, and the surrounding countryside.
*   **Character Customization:** Shops are available throughout the world where you can spend your earnings on bikes, clothes, hairstyles, tattoos, and various accessories, including "dirty magazines."

## Mission Structure and Progression
For those looking to focus on the narrative, *Agefield High: Rock the School* offers a structured mission path. The game includes 32 main missions that drive the core story forward. According to the developer, these missions lead to two different possible endings, though specific details regarding the choices or triggers required to reach these conclusions have not been disclosed.

In addition to the main storyline, there are 15 side missions and activities available for players to engage in. Whether you are focusing on the "Rock the School" plan or exploring the town to complete side objectives, there is plenty of content to keep you occupied throughout your playthrough.

## Technical Scope
The game is built as a small open-world experience. The map is designed to be dense rather than expansive, focusing on key locations that define the high school experience. By incorporating specific locales—such as the school campus, local neighborhoods, the town center, and the countryside—Refugium Games aims to provide a cohesive setting that reflects the early 2000s era.
