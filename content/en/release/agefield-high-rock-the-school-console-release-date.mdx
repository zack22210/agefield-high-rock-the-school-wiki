## What Is Agefield High: Rock the School?

*Agefield High: Rock the School* is a coming-of-age adventure game that transports players back to the early 2000s. The game is heavily inspired by the raunchy teen comedies that defined the era. Players step into the role of Sam, a new student at Agefield High School, as he navigates the complexities of friendship, romance, and rebellion.

The core gameplay centers on a plan devised by Sam’s new friends, Kale and Axel, to "Rock the School" and leave a lasting legacy before graduation. The experience is designed to last approximately 8–10 hours, blending narrative storytelling with a structured school and time system.

## Gameplay Features and World Exploration

Beyond the core narrative, the game offers a small open-world environment that encourages exploration. Players can roam through several distinct areas, including:

*   The Agefield High School campus
*   Two local neighborhoods
*   The town center
*   The surrounding countryside

The game balances its linear mission structure with free-roaming activities. Players must attend classes, with five different subjects available: English, Math, Geography, German, and Music. These activities are integrated into a time-based system, meaning your schedule at Agefield High will dictate your daily progression.

## Missions and Customization

The game provides a variety of objectives to keep players busy throughout their 8–10 hour playthrough. According to official developer information, the content includes:

*   **32 Main Missions:** These missions drive the primary narrative forward and feature two different endings depending on your choices and actions.
*   **15 Side Missions/Activities:** These provide additional opportunities to interact with the world and characters.
*   **Customization:** Players can visit various shops to purchase items such as bikes, clothing, hairstyles, tattoos, and accessories. There are even "dirty magazines" available for purchase, leaning into the game's early-2000s teen comedy aesthetic.

While there are two distinct endings to the main story, the developer has not released specific details regarding the triggers or requirements for these paths. Players will need to navigate the school year to discover how their choices impact the final outcome.
