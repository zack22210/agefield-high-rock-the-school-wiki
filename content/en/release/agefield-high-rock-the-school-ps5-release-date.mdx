## Agefield High: Rock the School Launch Details

Refugium Games announced the arrival of *Agefield High: Rock the School* for August 12, 2026. The title is a coming-of-age adventure that leans heavily into the aesthetics and atmosphere of the early 2000s. Players take on the role of Sam, a new student at Agefield High, as he navigates the complexities of teenage life, friendship, and rebellion.

The core narrative revolves around Sam’s partnership with two other students, Kale and Axel. Together, they aim to execute a plan titled "Rock the School" to cement their status as local legends before graduation. The game is inspired by raunchy teen comedies of the era, focusing on themes of winning over a "dream girl" and rebelling against school authority.

## Confirmed Gameplay Features and Scope

According to official developer specifications, *Agefield High: Rock the School* offers a focused single-player experience. The game is designed to provide approximately 8 to 10 hours of gameplay, depending on how much time the player spends exploring the town of Agefield.

### Mission Structure and Endings
The game features a structured narrative supported by a variety of tasks:
*   **Main Missions:** There are 32 main missions that drive the story of Sam, Kale, and Axel.
*   **Branching Paths:** The developer has confirmed that the story features two different endings, though the specific triggers or choices that lead to these endings have not been disclosed.
*   **Side Content:** Players can engage in 15 side missions and activities to further explore the town and its characters.

### The School System
A central mechanic of the game is the **Time & School System**. Unlike traditional open-world games where time may be static, *Agefield High* requires school attendance. Players must attend five different lesson types:
1.  English
2.  Math
3.  Geography
4.  German
5.  Music

### Customization and Shops
To lean into the "rebel" theme, the developer has included various shops throughout Agefield. Players can spend their time or resources on:
*   **Transportation:** Bikes are available for purchase to traverse the open world more quickly.
*   **Personalization:** Shops offer clothes, hairstyles, tattoos, and accessories to change Sam's appearance.
*   **Misc Items:** The game includes era-appropriate items, including "dirty magazines," which reflect the game's inspiration from raunchy teen comedies.
