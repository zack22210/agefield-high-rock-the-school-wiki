## What is Agefield High: Rock the School?

*Agefield High: Rock the School* is a coming-of-age adventure game that draws inspiration from the raunchy teen comedies of the early 2000s. The game places you in the role of Sam, a new student at Agefield High, as you navigate the complexities of high school life, friendship, and rebellion just as graduation approaches.

The core gameplay loop centers on helping your new friends, Kale and Axel, execute their "Rock the School" plan. Throughout the experience, you are tasked with balancing your academic responsibilities with your social life, all while attempting to leave your mark as a legend at the school.

## Gameplay Features and Scope

Refugium Games has designed the experience to offer a contained, narrative-driven adventure. According to the developer, the game features between 8 and 10 hours of gameplay. This runtime is packed with a variety of activities that define the daily life of a student in the town of Agefield:

*   **Mission Structure:** The game includes 32 main missions that feature two different possible endings, and 15 side missions or activities to complete.
*   **Academic System:** A core component of the game is the Time and School System. You must attend classes, which include five different subjects: English, Math, Geography, German, and Music.
*   **Open-World Exploration:** While the scope is described as a "small open-world," players are free to explore the school campus, two distinct neighborhoods, the town center, and the surrounding countryside.
*   **Customization and Shops:** You can visit various shops to purchase a wide array of items, including bikes, clothing, hairstyles, tattoos, and accessories.
