## What is Included in the Game?

When considering the value of the game, it is important to look at the features confirmed by the developer. *Agefield High: Rock the School* is inspired by early 2000s teen comedies like *American Pie* and games like *Bully*. It offers a specific set of mechanics and content designed for fans of that era.

### Core Gameplay Features
The game includes a variety of systems that define the high school experience in the town of Agefield:

*   **Main Story:** 32 main missions focusing on Sam, Kale, and Axel as they attempt to "Rock the School" before graduation.
*   **Multiple Endings:** The game features two different endings based on the choices you make, specifically regarding the prom.
*   **Side Activities:** 15 side missions and activities are scattered throughout the world.
*   **School System:** A complex time and school system requires you to attend classes (English, Math, Geography, German, and Music) or choose to skip them to earn cash elsewhere.
*   **Customization:** Players can change Sam’s outfits, hairstyles, and tattoos, and purchase items like bikes and accessories at local shops.

### The Open World
Unlike massive sandbox games, Agefield is a "handcrafted indie town" with a focus on personality over scale. The map includes:
*   The Agefield High School campus.
*   Two residential neighborhoods.
*   A town center.
*   The surrounding countryside.

## Gameplay and Combat Style

If you are deciding whether to purchase the game once the price is live, consider the gameplay style. The developers have stated that the combat is intentionally straightforward, avoiding complex "fifty-button combo systems." This old-school approach is designed to mirror the simplicity of games from the early 2000s.

The experience is strictly single-player. There are no multiplayer modes or social sandboxes; the focus remains entirely on Sam's personal journey and his rebellion alongside his friends Kale and Axel.

## Content Warning and Tone

Before purchasing, players should be aware of the game's specific tone. Refugium Games describes the content as "raunchy" and "edgy," intentionally evoking the humor of the early 2000s. The Steam page notes that the game contains:

*   Crude and "outdated" humor.
*   Mild violence without blood.
*   References to alcohol and sexual innuendo.
*   Partial nudity and sexual harassment themes.

This style of humor is a core part of the game’s identity, described by the developers as a tribute to the "American Pie-era." If you do not enjoy raunchy teen comedy, this title may not be for you regardless of the eventual price.
