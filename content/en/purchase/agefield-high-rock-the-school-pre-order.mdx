## What is Agefield High: Rock the School?

*Agefield High: Rock the School* is a single-player coming-of-age action-adventure game set in the year 2002. The story captures the rebellious energy and aesthetic of early 2000s teen comedies, complete with a vibrant color palette and an energetic pop-punk soundtrack. 

Players step into the shoes of Sam Tatum, a new kid who has just moved to the quirky town of Agefield with his nagging mother and annoying sister following his parents' split. Navigating a new school is tough, but Sam quickly teams up with two classmates:
* **Caleb "Kale" Bates:** A rebellious teen who firmly believes that "college is for chumps."
* **Axel Foley:** A passionate, wannabe rockstar trying to survive senior year.

Together, the trio devises the "Rock the School" plan. Their ultimate goal is to avoid graduating as "losers," win over their dream girls, and pull off a legendary senior year by rocking the prom. Along the way, they must contend with overbearing parents, nosey neighbors, perverted teachers, and Seth—the school's most popular and hostile jock.

While press and player previews frequently compare the open-world campus environment to classic titles like Rockstar's *Bully*, *Agefield High* is an entirely independent creation from Refugium Games.

## Gameplay Mechanics: School, Combat, and Exploration

The gameplay of *Agefield High: Rock the School* is split between structured daily school routines and after-school open-world exploration. The developer promises approximately 8 to 10 hours of gameplay.

### The Time and School System
During weekdays, Sam must attend class until the afternoon. The game features a complex school system with five distinct lesson types:
* English
* Math
* Geography
* German
* Music

If you choose to rebel, you can skip class entirely. However, doing so means you must actively evade school authorities and town police while causing chaos in the streets.

### Combat and Defense
Sam isn't a bully, but he is fully capable of defending himself against the town's hostile jocks. The combat system features:
* Basic punches and leg kicks.
* Defensive shuffles to break an opponent's guard.
* A dedicated block mechanic to mitigate incoming damage.

### Customization and Side Activities
The small open world consists of the Agefield High campus, two distinct residential neighborhoods, the town center, and the surrounding countryside. Because Sam doesn't have a car, you can visit Mike's Bike Shop to buy a bicycle. 

Throughout the town, various shops allow you to customize Sam's appearance with new clothes, hairstyles, and tattoos. You can also earn extra money by completing 15 different side missions, such as purchasing a "Play With" adult magazine and reselling the pages to classmates at a markup.
