If you are looking to experience a nostalgic, raunchy coming-of-age adventure, **Agefield High: Rock the School on Steam** offers a unique journey into the early 2000s. Developed by Refugium Games, this single-player title captures the spirit of teen comedies from that era, focusing on the story of a student named Sam navigating the challenges of high school life, friendship, and rebellion.

### What Is Agefield High: Rock the School?

*Agefield High: Rock the School* is an open-world indie game that transports players back to the early 2000s. You step into the shoes of Sam, a new student who moves to the town of Agefield just as graduation approaches. Upon arrival, you meet two students, Kale and Axel, who recruit you into their plan to "Rock the School" and leave a lasting mark on the institution before they graduate.

The game is designed as a single-player experience. It is not an expansive AAA sandbox, but rather a smaller, handcrafted indie world that emphasizes personality and detail over sheer size. Critics and press outlets, including IGN and GameSpot, have noted that the game wears its inspirations—specifically early 2000s teen movies like *American Pie*—on its sleeve, featuring edgy humor and a distinct cultural aesthetic.

### Gameplay and Mechanics

The gameplay in *Agefield High* is intentionally straightforward. It avoids complex, modern multi-button combo systems, favoring an old-school approach. Players are tasked with balancing their school attendance with a desire to rebel.

*   **Time and School System:** On weekdays, you are expected to be in class until the afternoon. However, the game allows you to choose whether to attend your lessons—which include subjects like English, Math, Geography, German, and Music—or skip them to explore the town.
*   **Exploration:** The game features a small open world consisting of the school campus, two distinct neighborhoods, the town center, and the surrounding countryside.
*   **Customization:** While it is not a deep RPG, you can customize your character, Sam, by changing his hairstyle, outfits, and even getting tattoos.
*   **Economy:** You can earn pocket money by performing well in your classes or by completing side missions. This currency can be used to purchase items like bikes, clothing, and accessories.

### Missions and Progression

The narrative arc centers on the "Rock the School" plan devised by Kale. The game provides a structured experience with a clear beginning and end.

*   **Main Story:** The main campaign spans approximately 8 to 10 hours of gameplay. It includes 32 main missions that lead to one of two different endings.
*   **Side Content:** Beyond the main plot, there are 15 side missions and activities available to keep you occupied as you navigate life in Agefield.
*   **Choices:** While you have the freedom to skip classes and engage in various activities, the most significant choice occurs toward the end of the game, specifically around the time of the school prom.

### Is This Game Right for You?

Because *Agefield High* leans heavily into the tropes of early 2000s teen comedies, it is important to understand the nature of its content. The developers have provided a clear content warning, noting that the game contains crude and edgy humor, mild violence without blood, alcohol consumption, sexual innuendo, sexual harassment, and partial nudity.

If you enjoy smaller, character-driven indie games and have a fondness for the aesthetic and tone of 2000s teen cinema, this title may align with your interests. However, if you are looking for a massive, feature-heavy AAA open world, a deep role-playing game, or a multiplayer experience, it is worth noting that *Agefield High* is a focused, single-player indie project.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Does Agefield High: Rock the School feature multiplayer?**
No, the game is a single-player experience from beginning to end. Every student you encounter is part of the game's AI-driven world.

**Is this an official sequel to Bully?**
No. While many players and press outlets have compared the game to *Bully* due to the high school setting and open-world structure, *Agefield High* is an independent project developed by Refugium Games.

**How many endings does the game have?**
There are two different endings available, which are determined by the choices you make during the game, specifically as you approach the prom.
